# Gem Guide — what to look for + a fun fact

Source of truth for gem content lives in [`hunt.json`](hunt.json) (that's what
`npm run seed` pushes to the database). This file keeps the full organizer-facing
guide, including facts too long for the in-app cards.

All gems are worth **1 pt** unless noted. Gem 17 has a **+1 bonus** for taking
the spiral staircase — award it manually in the admin review (type 2 and approve).

---

## Main Green & Front Green

### 1. Slavery Memorial — Martin Puryear, 2014 · Front Green · 1 pt
**Look for:** a broken cast-iron chain rising from a half-buried dome.
**Fun fact:** links break at eye level and the dome mirrors the sky. A moment of
quiet contemplation; photos aren't required.

### 2. Advice from a Former Student — Nina Katchadourian, 2010 · Faunce, near reception · 1 pt
**Look for:** an abstract ear symbol on the wall; two speakers loop clips of advice.
**Fun fact:** 800 snippets of wisdom from Brown alumni, classes 1939–2010.

### 3. Idee di Pietra (Ideas of Stone) — Giuseppe Penone, 2006 · Main Green · 1 pt
**Look for:** a 27-ft bronze tree cradling a 5,000-lb granite river stone in its branches.
**Fun fact:** modeled on a nut tree; shown at dOCUMENTA (13) in 2012.

### 4. Brown Bear 🐻 — Eli Harvey, 1922 · Main Green, near Faunce Arch · 1 pt
**Look for:** the 7-ft bronze Bruno on a tall pedestal.
**Fun fact:** funded by a "Put Hair on the Bear" drive that capped student gifts
at $1; the pedestal holds slate Roger Williams reportedly stepped on in 1636.

### 5. Casey Shearer Memorial — Howard Ben Tré, 2003 · Main Green (Casey Shearer '00 Courtyard) · 1 pt
**Look for:** a fountain of stacked bronze beads and glass; water trickles bead to bead.
**Fun fact:** honors a beloved Class of 2000 student and sports broadcaster.

### 6. Reclining Figure No. 2 (Bridge Prop) — Henry Moore, 1963 · Main Green · 1 pt
**Look for:** a three-part bronze reclining figure (often climbed on).
**Fun fact:** inspired by London's Waterloo Bridge.

## Ruth Simmons Quad

### 7. Marcus Aurelius — 1908 · Ruth Simmons Quad, behind Sayles Hall · 1 pt
**Look for:** a bronze equestrian statue facing Thayer St through Soldiers Memorial Gate.
**Fun fact:** a copy of the original on Capitoline Hill in Rome.

### 8. War Memorial — Richard Fleischner, 1997 · Ruth Simmons Quad, near Soldier's Arch · 1 pt
**Look for:** a bronze lattice with a marble slab of names, roses growing through it.
**Fun fact:** honors 205 Brown alumni lost in WWII, Korea, and Vietnam.

## Science quad & engineering

### 9. 1 1/2 — Carla Lavatelli · Science Plaza, in front of the SciLi · 1 pt
**Look for:** a 9-ft abstract sculpture with circular cut-outs on a black granite base.
**Fun fact:** weighs 7,000+ lbs; unveiled at 1985 Commencement.

### 10. America One — Dušan Džamonja · entrance of the CIT (Watson Center), next to SciLi · 1 pt
**Look for:** two interlocking silver-and-dark metal discs/spheres.
**Fun fact:** by a Croatian sculptor known for circular, WWII-themed works.

### 11. Infinite Possibility — Brown STEAM students, 2019 · lawn in front of the Engineering Research Center · 1 pt
**Look for:** a polished stainless-steel Möbius-strip sundial.
**Fun fact:** a "noon mark" — at noon a bead of light traces the sun's yearly
path; first student-designed piece approved by Brown's Public Art Committee.

## South campus

### 12. Swearer Bear 🐻 — Nicholas Swearer, 1988 · Maddock Alumni Center garden · 1 pt
**Look for:** a 5'6" standing bronze bear.
**Fun fact:** look inside the mouth — it's actually a person in a bear suit.

### 13. Little Bear Fountain 🐻 — 1937 · next to the Brown Faculty Club, Magee St · 1 pt
**Look for:** a bronze bear perched atop a fountain, slightly off the main campus.
**Fun fact:** a replica of a bear fountain the donor found in Breslau (now Wrocław).

## The Walk & north campus

### 14. Circle Dance — Tom Friedman, 2010 · The Walk, Waterman St (across from BERT) · 1 pt
**Look for:** silvery figures dancing in a ring.
**Fun fact:** cast from a maquette made of aluminum roasting pans; inspired by
Matisse's La Danse.

### 15. Guardian Birds — Façade of Robinson Hall, 64 Waterman St · 1 pt
**Look for:** carved stone winged creatures on the building's exterior.
**Fun fact:** Robinson Hall (1878) is on the National Register of Historic Places.

### 16. Pembroke Tribute Garden — steps leading to Pembroke Campus, by Pembroke Hall · 1 pt
**Look for:** a brick-and-granite monument with a bronze map/plaque.
**Fun fact:** honors the Women's College (1891) and the 1971 Pembroke–Brown merger.

### 17. Lines of Sight — Diane Samuels, 2006 · Sidney Frank Hall (Meeting St entrance, left then right) · 1 pt · spiral staircase = +1
**Look for:** glass pedestrian-bridge walls collaged with 600,000+ cut-glass
shapes and engraved quotations.
**Fun fact:** faint hands, enlarged 85x, are sandblasted the length of the
bridge. Take the spiral staircase for an extra point.

## Hope St & Lindemann

### 18. Posture Mirror — Nelson Fitness Center, 1st floor beyond reception · 1 pt
**Look for:** an elegant antique wood-framed full-length mirror in the hall.
**Fun fact:** from the 1912 women's "correction room"; used until 1971.

### 19. Indomitable 🐻 — Nicky Bibby, 2013 · Ittleson Quad, in front of Nelson Fitness Center (Hope St) · 1 pt
**Look for:** a towering standing bronze Kodiak (bear ~10 ft; piece ~14 ft).
**Fun fact:** Brown's most recent mascot bear, cast with the lost-wax process.

### 20. Infinite Composition — Leo Villareal, 2023 · Lindemann Performing Arts Center Lobby, 144 Angell St · 1 pt
**Look for:** 58 LED light pillars pulsing with programmed patterns.
**Fun fact:** custom software evokes swirling galaxies, tides, and fireflies.
